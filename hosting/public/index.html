Questions

Is Color Magic free?

Yes, it's free to try. New users get free credits for their first days, and can earn more by choosing to watch a short ad. Credit packs and a Premium subscription are available for families who color a lot; prices are shown in Google Play before you buy.

What age is it for?

Everyone aged 4 and up. Young children should use it with a parent or guardian nearby. Sign-in, purchases and contacting support are for adults.

Does it work offline?

Coloring and the saved gallery work offline. Creating a new coloring page needs an internet connection.

What if a picture isn't right?

Pictures are made by AI and checked by safety filters, but no filter is perfect. Tap Report this picture, or email us, and we'll review it.

How do I delete our account and data?

In the app, go to Parents → Delete Account, or follow the steps on the delete account page.
