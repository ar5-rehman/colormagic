# Delete your ColorMagic Kids account and data

**ColorMagic Kids** — by **ZyloMind**

You can ask us to delete your ColorMagic Kids account and the data linked to it at any
time.

## How to request deletion

1. **In the App (easiest):** open **Parent Area → Help & Support** and send a message
   saying **"Delete my ColorMagic data"**. Messages sent from the App automatically
   identify your account — this is the only way to identify a guest account that never
   used Google Sign-In.
2. **By email:** write to **abdurzylomind@gmail.com** with the subject
   **"Delete my ColorMagic data"** and include the Google account email you signed in
   with.
3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner.

We complete deletion **within 30 days** and let you know when it is done (if you gave us an email address).

## What we delete

- Your account (anonymous or Google-linked)
- Google account details we stored: email address, display name, profile photo URL
- Credit balances, subscription status, and coloring streaks
- Parent control settings
- Prompts and coloring pages stored on our servers
- Support messages linked to your account

## What we keep, and for how long

- **Purchase records** needed for tax, accounting, or legal reasons, for the period the
  law requires.
- **Crash reports** are kept for about 90 days, and **analytics events** for up to 14
  months. Neither contains your name or email address.

## Artwork on your device

Artwork, badges, and rewards saved **on your device** are not stored by us. Delete them
in the App (**Settings → Delete All Artwork**) or by uninstalling the App. Pictures you
saved to your phone's photo gallery stay there until you delete them.

## Subscriptions

Deleting your data does **not** cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the
**Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
